# Penguin

Pip is a plump little slate-blue penguin who takes typing very seriously. Head and body are one
soft egg (no neck crease: the head outline runs into the body's sides), with a white heart-shaped
face mask that flows straight into the belly, a tiny orange beak, rosy cheeks, a two-feather tuft
on top and orange feet that peek out under the keyboard. The flippers are short and chubby and tap
the keys with earnest, determined little pats; when a burst of typing goes well they flap. The
back is a deep blue-gray (not black) with one shade tone and a light sheen on the upper left, so
the silhouette still reads against the dark vibe and the dark keyboard.

Colors: `fur_dark` = slate back/head/flippers, `fur_dark_shade` = its shade, `fur_main` = the light
sheen on the head and flipper tips, `fur_light` = belly and face mask, `fur_light_shade` = belly
shade, `feature` = beak and feet. `fur_shade` is unused. Accessory colors are fixed literals.

All layers are generated by `_src/gen_penguin.py` (run it, then `scripts/render_art.py penguin`).

- `idle`: content "— —" eyes, closed beak, flippers resting on F/J.
- `peek`: same with small open eyes (the only open-eyed frame); uses `idle_paws`.
- `type_left` / `type_right` / `type_both`: one flipper pressed and squashed, the other lifted.
- `excited`: happy "^ ^" eyes, open beak, both flippers up and flared outward (flapping).
- `sleep`: head sinks into the shoulders (shared sleep transform), droopy "u" eyes.
- `wake`: head still lowered, left eye open, right eye sleepy; uses `sleep_paws`.
- `hold`: content eyes, flippers hug the item at the shared hold anchor.
- `sip`: happy eyes, flippers raise the item so the rim/straw meets the beak.
- `icon` / `icon_template`: face only; the template keeps the heart mask as the cut-out with eyes
  and beak solid inside it, so it still reads as a penguin at 16 px.

Accessories (`acc/`): `headphones` refit to the ear-less head (band sits right on the skull so it
stays inside the safe box); `beanie` (coral knit), `party_hat` (tilted onto the upper-left of the
head), `bow` (upper right), `glasses` (round red frames), each with a `_sleep` twin. `hold_*` keep
the shared hold anchors. `hold_*_sip.svg` are the sip positions (coffee translate(148 126)
rotate(-20), boba translate(148 136) rotate(-20), book translate(154 134) rotate(-12)): the shared
renderer currently uses `hold_<item>` for both `hold` and `sip`, so the sip preview on the sheet
shows the cup at the hold position until the app/renderer picks up a sip variant.
