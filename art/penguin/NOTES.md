# Penguin: Pip

Pip is a small, very earnest penguin who treats every keystroke like an important job. One soft
slate-navy egg with no neck: the head flows straight into the body, a white heart-shaped face mask
runs down into a big belly, and there's a little orange beak, rosy cheeks, two curly feathers on the
crown and big orange feet that poke out under the keyboard. The flippers are short, lighter-slate
paddles that hang from the sides and just reach the back corners of a smaller keyboard set low in
front of the belly. Pip can't reach far, so the board comes to Pip. When the typing gets fast the
tuft springs up and both flippers fly out in a happy flap.

Everything is generated by `_src/gen_penguin.py` (`python3 art/penguin/_src/gen_penguin.py`, then
`python3 scripts/render_art.py penguin`). Edit the generator, not the SVGs. Head ellipse
(138, 86), rx 63, ry 54. The keyboard is scaled 0.76 and moved (30, 8) (`anchors.json`). Because
head and body are one silhouette, sleep doesn't rotate a head group. A smooth warp (`sleep_w`)
sinks and spreads the top of the egg (nothing below y 168 moves), and the face, tuft and every
`_sleep` head item go through the same warp, so they stay fitted.

Palette tokens: `fur_main` navy back, `fur_shade` its shade, `fur_light`/`fur_light_shade` the
white face and belly, `feature` orange beak and feet. Two tokens are used for penguin-specific tones:
`fur_dark` is the *lighter* slate sheen (crown highlight and the flippers, which keeps the navy
readable on the dark vibe), and `fur_dark_shade` is the orange shade on the feet and beak.

- `idle`: content "— —" eyes, closed beak with a tiny smile split, flipper tips resting on the
  back-row keys at both ends of the board.
- `peek`: same with small round open eyes. Uses `idle_paws`.
- `type_left`: L flipper pressed (tip down 3.5, small contact mark under it), R flipper lifted.
- `type_right`: mirror of `type_left`.
- `type_both`: both flippers pressed.
- `excited`: "^ ^" eyes, open beak with a pink tongue, tuft perked, both flippers flung up and out
  (flapping). Ships its own `acc/motion.svg` with marks around the flipper tips. The body stays put
  so head items stay fitted; the app's bob adds the hop.
- `sleep`: head tucked down into the chest (egg top sunk ~27 units), "u" sleepy eyes, flippers
  still on the keys. z's offset (2, 6).
- `wake`: tucked like sleep, left eye open, right eye sleepy. Uses `sleep_paws`.
- `hold`: flippers curl in and scoop the item against the chest (`acc/hold_*`, item at
  (142, 140)), clipped above the keyboard's back edge.
- `sip`: happy eyes, both flippers raised to hold the cup/boba at the beak (`acc/sip_*`); the book
  is held a little lower at the chest so the eyes stay visible above it.
- `icon` / `icon_template`: front-facing round face with tuft, white heart mask, beak and cheeks;
  outline 16, eyes 18. The template cuts out the eyes and beak.

Accessories (`acc/`, each with a `_sleep` twin run through the same warp): `headphones` (band over
the crown with the tuft poking up behind it, cups where ears would be, far cup as a crescent),
`beanie` (red knit with a cream pompom, covers the tuft), `party_hat` (blue, perched on the crown),
`bow` (pink, right of the tuft), `glasses` (round, on the white face). Own desk copies:
`desk_lamp` moved left and `desk_plant` moved right/down so they peek out beside the tall egg,
`desk_mug` moved 3 right.
