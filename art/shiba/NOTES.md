# Shiba: Kinako

Kinako is a red shiba who is quietly very pleased with himself. He sits upright behind the keyboard
with his ears up and a smug little "w" smile under a small dark nose, eyes shut in a content
"— —" as if every keystroke was his idea. Classic urajiro markings: cream cheeks and muzzle that
rise in two soft humps under the eyes, cream eyebrow dots, cream bib and cream socks (paws), and a
plush curled tail peeking out over his back on the left, away from the plant and mug. Fast typing
makes him beam: "^ ^" eyes and an open grin with a tiny pink tongue. Confident, cheerful, cozy.

All layers are generated by `_src/gen_shiba.py` (`pip install shapely`, then
`python3 art/shiba/_src/gen_shiba.py` and `python3 scripts/render_art.py shiba`). Edit the
generator, not the SVGs. Outlines are hand-placed beziers; patches that must stay inside them
(mask, bib, shade crescents, chin-trimmed forearms, ear tops redrawn over head items) are computed
with shapely. Head ≈ center (153, 82), rx 59, ry 46 (`anchors.json`); keyboard at the shared default.

- `idle`: content closed eyes, smug "w" smile, chubby forearms bowed outward, cream paws on F/J.
- `peek`: same with round open eyes (larger than the minimum so they read) and a shine dot. Uses `idle_paws`.
- `type_left`: L paw pressed (squashed, +3), R paw raised (+2, −10, untilted).
- `type_right`: R paw pressed, L paw raised.
- `type_both`: both paws pressed.
- `excited`: "^ ^" eyes, open grin with a tiny tongue, both paws lifted 12; shared motion marks.
- `sleep`: head lowered by `translate(-4 12) rotate(-6 152 80)`, sleepy "u" eyes (distinct from the
  flat content eyes), relaxed mouth; forearms trimmed to the lowered chin.
- `wake`: head still lowered, left eye open, right eye sleepy. Uses `sleep_paws`.
- `hold`: content face, forearms curl in and the paws hug the item at the shared hug anchor
  (shared `hold_*` positions fit, so no `acc/hold_*` overrides).
- `sip`: happy closed eyes, small pursed mouth (hidden behind the cup), paws raised to ≈ (138,130)/(172,124). Item
  positions are `acc/sip_coffee.svg` (`translate(146 114) rotate(-25)`), `acc/sip_boba.svg`
  (`translate(147 116) rotate(-22)`, straw at the mouth) and `acc/sip_book.svg`
  (`translate(153 127) rotate(-8)`, held up to read under the nose).
- `icon` / `icon_template`: the face with ears enlarged 25% about their bases, outline 16, eyes 18,
  cheek tuft dropped; reads as an orange-and-cream shiba face at 16 px.

Accessories (`acc/`, each with a `_sleep` twin in the sleep transform): `headphones` refit to the
head (near cup at the right edge, far-cup crescent on the left) with the band running *behind* the
upright ears: the headphones layer redraws the visible ear tops over the band. `beanie` (berry
knit) sits on the skull between the ears, which are redrawn in front of it the same way.
`party_hat` is a small cone between the ears, `bow` sits at the base of the left ear, `glasses`
are round specs with the temple running back toward the near ear.
