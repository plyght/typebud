# Shiba: Kinako

Kinako is a red shiba who sits bolt upright behind the keyboard, chest puffed out and a little too
pleased with herself, as if every keystroke were her own idea. She has a fox-like wedge face: a round
crown, fluffy cheek tufts and a soft muzzle bump. Her tall triangular ears are set wide, and the
iconic tight "cinnamon roll" curl of a tail lies on her back over the right flank. The bold cream
urajiro covers her cheeks, muzzle, eyebrow dots, neck ruff, socks and the inside of the tail curl,
with the orange running down to the nose in a "V". The default face is a smug, content "— —" over a
dark nose and a wide smile that curls up at the corners. When typing gets fast she breaks into a big
open grin with her tongue out, and the tail wags.

All layers are generated by `_src/gen_shiba.py` (`python3 art/shiba/_src/gen_shiba.py`, then
`python3 scripts/render_art.py shiba`). Edit the generator, not the SVGs. Head ellipse (anchors.json)
is centred at (148, 86), rx 70, ry 48. The keyboard sits 6 units lower than the shared default
(`translate [0, 6]`) so the upright chest and ruff show above it. The sleep head transform is
`translate(-3 30) rotate(-8 148 84)`.

- `idle`: content eyes, smug smile, both front paws on the keys (cream socks), tail curl at rest.
- `peek`: same with small round open eyes. Uses `idle_paws`.
- `type_left`: L paw pressed, R paw raised showing pink toe beans; the curl tips out a little.
- `type_right`: R paw pressed, L paw raised; the curl tips in.
- `type_both`: both paws pressed; curl at rest.
- `excited`: "^ ^" eyes, big open grin with tongue, ears perked, both paws up, curl swung out. Its
  own `acc/motion.svg` adds two wag marks beside the curl to the shared paw dashes.
- `sleep`: head dropped onto the paws (sleep transform), "u" eyes; paws tucked closer together under
  the chin, curl relaxed inward.
- `wake`: head still down, left eye open, right eye sleepy. Uses `sleep_paws`.
- `hold`: content face, forearms hugging the item in front of the ruff (`acc/hold_*`: shared drawings
  at `translate(152 146)`, just above the keyboard's back edge).
- `sip`: happy closed eyes, item raised to the muzzle (`acc/sip_*`: `translate(135 124) rotate(-20)`).
- `icon` / `icon_template`: dedicated front face with tall ears, cheek tufts, brow dots, cream mask
  and big nose. The template cuts out the eyes, brow dots, inner ears and the whole cream mask, and
  leaves the nose and smile as islands, so at 16 px it reads as a shiba, not a cat.

Accessories (`acc/`, each with a `_sleep` twin in the sleep transform): `headphones` with the band
running behind both tall ears; `beanie` (a dusty-blue knit with two pointed ear pockets, so the
upright ears stay up inside it); `party_hat` (between the ears); `bow` (at the base of the right ear);
`glasses`. `desk_plant` is a raised copy whose leaves rise above the tail curl.
