# Capybara

**Personality.** Mochi-level calm. A chunky caramel barrel with a boxy loaf of a head, tiny round
ears perched on the top corners, and a big blunt dark muzzle that takes up the lower face, with a
little "ω" mouth under two slanted nostrils. One swoopy cowlick sticks up from the crown and never
lies flat (it shows over the headphone band too). Nothing bothers this capybara: eyes stay softly
shut while it types, it opens them only for a quick look around, and its idea of excitement is a
small open-mouthed grin. The forearms are short, chubby capsules with a rounded elbow and a shaded
underside, so they read as cuddly arms and not sticks. The optional `acc/yuzu.svg` puts a yuzu on
its head, like a capybara in a hot spring.

## Frames
- `idle`: content "— —" eyes, calm "ω" mouth, paws resting on F/J.
- `peek`: same as idle with bigger open eyes (rx 4.8, ry 5.8) and a white shine, so the quick look reads at 96 px.
- `type_left` / `type_right` / `type_both`: same body/head as idle; paws press (squash, drop 3) or lift (10 up, no tilt).
- `excited`: happy "^ ^" eyes, small open "D" mouth in `feature` pink, both paws lifted 12.
- `sleep`: head in the sleep transform, droopy "u" eyes (different from the content lines), slack mouth.
- `wake`: head still lowered, left eye open, right eye still a sleepy arc.
- `hold`: content face, arms curled around the held item's lower front.
- `sip`: blissful "^ ^" eyes, little pursed "o" mouth at the rim/straw, paws lifting the item.
- `icon` / `icon_template`: front-ish face drawn for 16 px: loaf head, ear nubs, eye slits, muzzle with nostrils.

## Files
- All SVGs are generated by `_src/gen.py` (`python3 art/capybara/_src/gen.py && python3 scripts/render_art.py capybara`).
  Edit the generator, not the SVGs.
- `acc/`: headphones, beanie, party_hat, bow, glasses (+ `_sleep`), fitted to the loaf head. The headphone band
  and the beanie sit behind the ears (the ears are redrawn on top, poking through). Optional extra: `yuzu` (+ `_sleep`).
- `acc/hold_{coffee,boba,book}.svg`: the shared items (hug position unchanged, so these files only make it explicit).
- `acc/hold_{coffee,boba,book}_sip.svg`: the same drawings at the **sip** position (rim/straw at the muzzle,
  everything above the keyboard back edge). The spec gives a sip anchor but no file name for it, so `<item>_sip`
  follows the `_sleep` naming. `scripts/render_art.py` doesn't load these yet, so the `sip` column in `sheet.png` shows
  the cup in the hug position.
