# Capybara: Yuzu

Yuzu is a long, low, completely unbothered capybara who types the way capybaras do everything:
lying down. It loafs belly-down behind the keyboard with its barrel body stretched out along the
board's back edge, a big blocky head turned a little toward you, and two stubby dark front feet
resting on the keys. The tall, blunt, slightly darker snout is the star of the face; the tiny
content eyes sit high and far back, the round ears are small and set on top, and a little cowlick
sticks up from the flat crown. Zen energy: nothing surprises Yuzu, though fast typing gets a rare
open-mouthed grin. No tail (capybaras barely have one); a folded hind leg and one dark hind foot
peek out by the keyboard's back-right corner.

All layers are generated by `_src/gen_capybara.py` (`python3 art/capybara/_src/gen_capybara.py`,
then `python3 scripts/render_art.py capybara`). Edit the generator, not the SVGs.

Geometry (see `anchors.json`): the keyboard is scaled to 0.94 and moved by (6, 4) so the long body
fits on the right; paw rest points L (92,172), R (134,182); head ellipse c (102,93), rx 66, ry 60.
Sleep transform for the head and its items: `translate(2 7) rotate(-6 128 96)` (snout dips toward
the keys). Because the body fills the right side, Yuzu ships its own `acc/desk_lamp.svg` (the
shared lamp mirrored to the back right, peeking over the back), `acc/desk_plant.svg` (moved to the
back left beside the snout), `acc/zzz.svg` (z's rise up-right over the back, since the head fills
the top left) and `acc/motion.svg` (dashes around its own raised feet). Music notes use the shared
layer with a small `overlays` offset.

- `idle`: content "— —" eyes, tiny "w" mouth under a short philtrum, both feet resting on the keys.
- `peek`: same with tiny open eyes (with shine). Uses `idle_paws`.
- `type_left`: L foot pressed (squashed, drops 3), R foot raised showing its dark pad.
- `type_right`: R foot pressed, L foot raised.
- `type_both`: both feet pressed.
- `excited`: "^ ^" eyes, small open mouth, both feet lifted and spread; own motion marks.
- `sleep`: head dips (sleep transform), "u" sleepy eyes; z's over the back.
- `wake`: head still dipped, far (left) eye open, near eye sleepy. Uses `sleep_paws`.
- `hold`: content face, the item held up against the chest just right of the snout with both front
  feet (`acc/hold_*`, group transform `translate(147 138) rotate(6)`; stays above the keys).
- `sip`: happy eyes, item raised to the mouth corner (`acc/sip_*`, `translate(100 126) rotate(-22)`).
- `icon` / `icon_template`: front face with the tall dark snout, small ears, outline 16, eyes 18.

Accessories (`acc/`, each with a `_sleep` twin in the sleep transform): `headphones` (far cup peeks
past the forehead, near cup over the side of the head), `beanie` (taller knit dome, tiny ears poke
out), `party_hat` (on the crown), `bow` (in front of the near ear), `glasses` (round, over the
high-set eyes). Bonus: `yuzu` / `yuzu_sleep`, a yuzu balanced on the head onsen-style; it follows
the head-item contract but is not in the app's head-item list yet.

Palette: warm caramel `fur_main`, one shade, a slightly darker `fur_dark` for snout, ears, front
legs and feet; `fur_light` tokens are unused and omitted.
